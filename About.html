The Educational Journey of Jaydon Joseph

I started the Brizan pre-school when I was three years old. There I spent 2 years developing my reading and writing abilities with others until we graduated in 2013.

After pre-school, I attended the Happy Hill primary school where we did various general subjects such as General education, Social Studies, Math, English, Physical Education, Information Technology etc. I performed to the best of my abilities all throughout kindergarten to Grade 6 until graduation in 2020.

When Primary school was over I applied to different secondary schools then waited to know which school I will get into. Some weeks later, we found out that i was accepted into one of my top two choices which was Presentation Brothers College. I started school during the covid era (2020) so all of our classes were online. I started school with primary school mentality of not really studying so I performed average during Forms 1 and 2. However, I began studying a bit more in form 3 and onwards resulting in a great improvement in my overall average at P.B.C. This entire Secondary school experience lasted for 5 years, meaning that graduation took place in 2025.

After CXC examinations and graduation, I took some time to figure out what I was going to do in college. Eventually my family and I decided to settle down for Information Technology.
